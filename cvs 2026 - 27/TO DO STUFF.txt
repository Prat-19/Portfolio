Build a "Lidl-style" retail KPI dashboard in Power BI. Pick a public retail dataset (e.g., the UK supermarket data, or a Kaggle retail dataset) and build a 1-page dashboard with turnover, gross margin, shrinkage/stock loss, ATV, sales-per-square-foot as the headline KPIs, plus a regional store-comparison view. Host it publicly (Power BI Public) and link to it from your CV and LinkedIn. This is the single most differentiated thing a controlling-track applicant can show.
